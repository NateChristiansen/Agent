# Lesson lessons

Read with `/lesson`. These incidents explain what must be recorded and what a public reader
can verify.

## Record a correction even when the state change is the whole fix

The owner noticed a feature still marked proposed with many tasks checked. Its status was
fixed, but no log or lesson followed; a retro reader found the correction a day later in raw
captures. When the owner corrects a status, mapping or tick, fix the state and record the
mistake through the routing loop. No reliable correction-shaped stop hook exists.

## Put public evidence in the text

Most `evidence:` fields in the retired playbook pointed to private records, and many
wikilinked into `features/` content that is absent in public clones. Replacing a private wikilink
with plain text did not make it accessible. State the measurable symptom or owner quote in
the public entry itself; keep private back-references in the private feature record.
